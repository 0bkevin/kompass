Verified 2026-10-18 UTC. Completed with fixes and bounded limitations.

The business is the actual 30X executive-education company: [30x.com](https://30x.com/) and the homepage-linked [@crece30x](https://www.instagram.com/crece30x/). ## Limits and cleanup

No actual Instagram posts, follower metrics, student interests, social trends, external publication or recruited-user usability result are claimed. This review adds current real-provider evidence for carousel, reel and newsletter; post/video local-format behavior is covered by the earlier independent format audit and existing regression suite, not regenerated here. A literal example label/section-heading check verifies structure, not the factual or editorial truth of the model's content; human review remains necessary.

During the coordinator's rebuild, the running old SSR process referenced removed hashed assets and showed two 404s on reload. Restarting the isolated host loaded the current manifest and restored the saved results. This was a coordinated build/restart artifact, retained as an operational limitation rather than counted as lost data or a new user-workflow defect. The expected reviewer write rejection returned 403. Immediate navigation aborted two obsolete insights reads; no saved data was lost.

The temporary source frontend, isolated production host and both owned QA tabs were stopped/closed after evidence capture. The coordinator's main servers, Google session and tabs were not changed by cleanup.
