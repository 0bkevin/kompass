# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Reuse the user-selected ../30x-track application: React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Start/Router, Express 5, Node 24 and SQLite. No existing production data or credentials are copied.

## Users

Companies and their content teams. Each company signs in to its own workspace and starts with its website, public social links and content it can provide.

## Product Purpose

Support the full path from company context and audience needs to discovery, an explained editorial opportunity, original content, review, planning and learning from recorded outcomes. The user explicitly requests a complete platform and a simple, guided experience with little cognitive load.

## Positioning

Connect audience needs to public evidence and a concrete content decision. Audience fit, growth and creative explanations must expose their evidence and uncertainty.

## Operating Context

Spanish web application for content teams, initially grounded in Latin America. Account, workspace, company context, audience, sources, opportunities, references, drafts, reviews, calendar and results. Existing multibrand isolation, roles and approval protection must be preserved.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The challenge requires public content, no individual audience profiles, no access behind third-party logins and no bypass of platform terms. Company website extraction and linked/imported content are different states. A public URL is not proof of successful extraction. Learner enrollment is not proof of following a creator, consuming content or membership of a representative audience.

Keep observations separate from editorial hypotheses. No invented metrics, global coverage, causal explanations or success predictions. Connected providers require real credentials; the deployment operator configures shared AI once, workspaces inherit it, and readiness requires both configuration and current access permission. User-facing company setup never requires provider keys. Absent providers expose the local editorial workflow and accurate status. Results can be registered manually and are labeled as such. Scheduling is not automatic publication.

## Brand Commitments

The application is named `kompass`, written in lowercase on every public and authenticated surface. This name replaces the previous product name across the application.

The user-selected promise is «Le damos dirección a tu contenido». Written content should explain the decisions the team can make: who to address, what question to answer, which sources support the topic, how to shape and review the piece, and what recorded results suggest trying next. Use concrete Spanish for content teams. Keep clear operational labels and factual limitations. Avoid vague growth promises, trend claims without evidence, motivational filler and repeated compass metaphors; the promise does not need to appear in every instruction.

The user explicitly authorizes a complete replacement visual identity for the public landing: dark by default with purple, informed by Resend and Exa. This scope starts only at the landing. The authenticated workspace and authentication retain their established restrained green, light neutral surfaces and familiar controls. Product truth and operational behavior remain binding across both worlds.

## Evidence on Hand

Prior application in ../30x-track; the audio transcript and challenge supplied in this conversation. There are no live operator credentials, real company audience datasets or verified loss/ROI figures supplied. Demo content must be explicitly illustrative.

## Product Principles

- One clear next action at each stage; detailed settings remain available when needed.
- Import first, review and correct second; avoid asking users to transcribe their website into a long form.
- Show what was observed, inferred, supplied by the team or unavailable.
- Keep company workspaces isolated and expensive operations explicit and bounded.
- Let users complete the editorial workflow and close the loop with results.

## Open Decisions

Deployment host, deadline and API operating budget are not supplied. Do not infer purchasing authorization. Private learner datasets are outside the current challenge scope. Broader personal-data use requires a changed brief.

## Acceso a fuentes revisado

La revisión de condiciones de uso aplica a cada finalidad, no solo a la disponibilidad pública de una URL. [La matriz vigente](docs/compliance/README.md) registra fuentes y funciones habilitadas, restringidas y sus condiciones. Apify Instagram/TikTok, Reddit, el análisis editorial con datos de YouTube y Gemini video están bloqueados. El catálogo público y los contadores oficiales de YouTube tienen un [conector separado](docs/official-social-sync-20261018.md); esos datos no se envían a la IA ni al aprendizaje editorial. No se promete cobertura social ni autorización por guardar una credencial. Webs/feeds adicionales, IA y webhooks requieren una revisión vigente; el recorrido con material propio y fuentes aprobadas sigue disponible.
